Somos Uma Geração Perdida?

Somos Uma Geração Perdida?

// Promoter & Tutor // Colective film // 2025 // O Meu Lugar no Mundo //

Colective film produced with the young students of Associação O Meu Lugar no Mundo in collaboration with the comunity of Anselmo Braamcamp street, in Porto.

The film follows a group of youngsters who want to organize a surprise party to one of the oldest residents of the street, Mrs. Esmeralda. They summon the entire neighborhood to join the celebration, inviting us to  discover this street we inhabit through the kino-eye of this youngsters. 

TRAILER

Project developed in co-production with the municipality of Porto through the program Cultura em Expansão.

Production and Artistic Direction: Diogo Machado Ferreira Francisco Moura Relvas Nuno Pires Paulo Mariz Vasco Monteiro

Pedagogic Direction: Cátia Oliveira
